# CDL Brain Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

CDL Brain: Exam Prep ("the app") is published by Zbyt Studios.

## Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

## Data stored on your device

Your quiz progress, scores and streak are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
